Final Test — Pre-IELTS Course

This test checks what you have learned throughout the course. It has 5 parts: 1) Vocabulary (25 questions), 2) Grammar (25 questions), 3) Reading (20 questions), and 4) Listening (20 questions) — these four parts are marked automatically out of a total of 90 points. Part 5 — Speaking will be marked separately by your teacher out of 10 points, giving a final total out of 100.

Instructions: Work through each part using the tabs at the top. You may move freely between parts and change your answers at any time before submitting. For the Listening part, please use headphones if possible and press the play button to listen — you may replay the audio as many times as you need. When you have finished all four parts, go to the last tab and press "Nộp bài — Submit Test" to see your results.

Good luck! 💪
